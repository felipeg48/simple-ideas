# See it move

A slide that turns like a cube. A number that rolls into place like an
odometer. A parcel that flies from the shop to the door. In si each of these
is **one line of Markdown**, and the page plays it itself: no library, no
timeline to drag, no video to export. It works in any browser, from a phone
to a 4K projector, and print, PDF and reduced motion show the slide at rest.

<!-- slides: features/showcase/transitions.md -->

Click the deck, then → and ←. Every card below has a **▶ Watch it** that
opens the moment on its own.

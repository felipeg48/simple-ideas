# si 3: demos that film like edited videos

Status: spec, decided with the founder on 2026-11-12. Planned as **v3.0.0**,
the release that completes web demos ("decks that present themselves, demos
that film themselves"). Builds on `si demo run` (v2.35–v2.42).

## 1. Transitions

Where a demo video cuts today: between features in `<script>.mp4` (title card
→ clip), when the scene switches inside a feature (`terminal` ↔ `browser`),
when `go` loads a page, when the region changes.

- **Default `fade` 0.4 s** at every one of those cuts (was a hard cut).
- Front matter `transition: fade` / `transition: slide 0.6s`; `--transition`;
  `transition: cut` restores the old look.
- Per step, after the step: `- browser · slide`, `- terminal "Client" · pop`,
  `- go /orders · fade`.
- Names: `cut`, `fade`, `slide` (`slide-left` default, `slide-right`,
  `slide-up`, `slide-down`), `wipe` (`wipe-left` …), `pop` (the new scene
  grows in from 92 % with a fade), `zoom` (zooms into the new scene),
  `circle` (opens from the cursor). Unknown names: a `si check` problem.
- The picture overlaps; the voice is never cut or sped up; chapters and
  captions stay on the output timeline.
- Built at encode time from the take's frames (ffmpeg `xfade` where it maps,
  frames drawn in Go where it does not), nothing in the page.

## 2. Camera moves

- `- zoom to "Agents panel"`: the picture glides from where it is to that
  element's box (with a margin), and stays.
- `- pan to "Footer"`: glides to the element at the current zoom.
- `- zoom out`: back to the whole page.
- `Region: ".agents" · zoom` (and `region: … · zoom`): the feature opens on
  the whole page and zooms into its region instead of starting cropped.
- `· 0.8s` sets the move's length (default 0.8 s, eased).
- Boxes are measured on the page when the step runs (they follow scrolling);
  the moves are a per-frame crop rectangle applied when the clip is encoded,
  so the output frame size never changes.

## 3. Spotlight (alias `focus`)

```markdown
- spotlight "Pay"                                # dim the rest to 60 %, rounded box around Pay
- spotlight "Pay" · circle
- spotlight "Agents panel" · label "Focus" · for 3s
- spotlight "Pay" · blur                         # blur the rest instead
- spotlight off
```

- Drawn by the in-page overlay (`overlay.js`), like notes: the dark layer
  fades in and the cut-out shrinks from the whole screen onto the element; it
  follows the element; `label "…"` is a tag on the cut-out's edge, in the
  notes' style.
- Ends at `spotlight off`, after `for Ns`, or with its `Say:` block.
- Works with `note` (the note sits on the dark layer), regions and zoom.
- In `si demo test`: checks the element exists, draws nothing.

## 4. The rest of the 3.0 list

- **`window` step**: `- window "si-presenter"` (or `- window 2`) switches the
  performed and filmed page to a popup or new tab the page opened (the
  teleprompter); `- window main` comes back. `si demo test` too.
- **`drag` step**: `- drag from "Shop" to "Doorstep"` (elements) or
  `- drag by 300,80` / `- drag from 400,300 to 900,420` (points), with the
  drawn cursor gliding and the button held: drawing on a slide (`a`), moving
  map topics, sliders.
- **Title cards keep the script's casing**: `What it does: si reads…` no
  longer becomes "Si reads".
- **docs/demos/presenting.md** gains the teleprompter (`window`) and drawing
  (`drag`); every demo script uses transitions, a zoom and a spotlight where
  they help; CI re-films them for the release.
- **"What's new in si 3"**: a page on the site and a section in the README
  (with the new videos), CHANGELOG v3.0.0.

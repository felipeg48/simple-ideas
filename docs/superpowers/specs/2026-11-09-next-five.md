# The next six: audience, handouts, recording, brand themes, co-presenters, the Theme editor

Status: specs written 2026-11-09; the founder wants all five, one at a time.

| # | Spec | What it gives | Size | Builds on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Audience mode](2026-11-09-audience-mode.md) | the room follows on phones, votes in live polls, sends questions | large | `--remote` relay, HTTPS, QR, teleprompter |
| 2 | [Handouts](2026-11-09-handouts.md) | `--handout`: slides + Points: (or lines, or a grid) as one PDF | small | `--pdf`, thumb view, `internal/qr` |
| 3 | [Record your talk](2026-11-09-record-your-talk.md) | narration + timeline → `--mp4 --narration`; a pace report | medium | teleprompter, real-motion `--mp4` |
| 4 | [Theme from a logo](2026-11-09-theme-from-logo.md) | `si theme from logo.png`: a contrast-checked light/dark theme | medium | theme tokens, contract.go, a11y suite |
| 5 | [Two presenters](2026-11-09-two-presenters.md) | `Notes[Ana]:`, per-speaker teleprompters, hand-over cues | medium | parser, teleprompter, `--remote` |
| 6 | [Theme editor in the Studio](2026-11-09-studio-theme-editor.md) | make and edit a theme with live preview, contrast checks and fixes, in the web Studio and the Mac app | large | Studio views, contract.go tokens, theme from a logo (4) |

Suggested order: 2 (small, used at once for courses) or 1 (the most
distinctive), then 3, 4, 5. The Theme editor (6) starts with engine fixes
(`extends`, the index page's theme) that help every CSS theme, and uses 4's
logo palette as one way to start, so 4 then 6 work well back to back. Each ships as its own minor release with RULES.md,
the manual, the site, the skill (regenerated) and an example used as content.

Every spec checks itself against the non-negotiable rules in CLAUDE.md, lists
its tests (unit, golden, e2e, and what only a real room or device can show),
and ends with open questions for the founder.

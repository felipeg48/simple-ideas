I grew up above my parents' removals company in Lisbon and have been
designing for people who carry boxes ever since. Before going freelance I
spent four years on the Northwind Logistics design team.

## What I do

<!-- cards: 3 -->

### Research · On the floor

Two days beside the people who will use it, before a single screen.

### Design · In the browser

Prototypes on the real device, in the real light, with gloves on.

### Hand-off · With the team

Specs, components and a week of pairing with the engineers.

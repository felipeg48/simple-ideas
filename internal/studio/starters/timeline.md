## Beginnings

### 1990 · The first idea
A sketch on a napkin: one sentence about what it should do.

### 1994 · A working prototype
Three people, one room, and a version that runs on a single machine.

## Growth

### 2001 · The first customers

### 2008 · Open to everyone
Anyone can sign up; the team grows to fifty.

## Today

### 2015 · A platform

### 2024 · The next chapter
A rewrite for the next ten years, kept compatible with the last ten.

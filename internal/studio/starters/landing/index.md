## Why warehouses switch

<!-- cards: 3 -->

### A live dock board · Plan · teal

Every door and every booking on one board. Drag a booking to another door
and the driver's phone updates at once.

### Late vans, handled · React · orange

A van ten minutes late turns orange; one that blocks the next booking turns
red, with the free doors beside it.

### Drivers who know · Arrive · hi

Each driver gets a text with the door and the time, and checks in by
replying. No queue at the gatehouse.

## Questions

<!-- accordion -->

### Does it work with our warehouse system?

Yes. Dockside reads bookings from any system that can export a CSV or call a
web hook, and writes departures back the same way.

### What do drivers need?

A phone that receives text messages. There is no app to install.

### How long does it take to set up?

One afternoon for a site with up to twenty doors. We do the first one with
you, on a call.

### What happens to our data if we leave?

You export everything as CSV at any time, and we delete it thirty days
after the last invoice.

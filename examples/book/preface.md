# Preface

Every order Northwind takes comes with a promise printed at checkout: delivered
by Thursday. This reference explains how an order keeps that promise, station
by station, and what the teams do when a station fails.

Read [](01-journey.md) first; the other chapters stand on their own. Terms in
the text such as ((idempotency key)) are collected in the [index](book-index.md).

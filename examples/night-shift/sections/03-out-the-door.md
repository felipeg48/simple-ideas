# Out the door

---

## Cool, then bag

- Onto the rack — listen for the **crackle** as it cools
- A full hour before cutting; early slices steam the crumb
- Bagged by the dozen, labelled by the batch

Notes: Bread keeps baking as it cools. Cutting too soon trades a clean crumb for a gummy one.

---

## From rack to counter

```stage
dir: LR
Cool --> Bag --> Load[Load the van] --> Shop
step Cool
step Bag
step Load
step ok Shop
```

- Walk it one step at a time; the batch is tracked to the shelf

Notes: Each click moves the batch one hand-off closer to the door.

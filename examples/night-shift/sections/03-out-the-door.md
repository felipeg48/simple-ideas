# Out the door

## Cool, then bag

- Onto the rack — listen for the **crackle** as it cools
- A full hour before cutting; early slices steam the crumb
- Bagged by the dozen, labelled by the batch

Notes: Bread keeps baking as it cools. Cutting too soon trades a clean crumb for a gummy one.

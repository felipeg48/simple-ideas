# Mix & rise

---

<!-- layout: two-col -->
## It starts at ten

- A spoon of last night's **levain**, awake and bubbling
- Flour, water, salt — and nothing else
- A long **autolyse** rest, then a slow, patient mix

<!-- column -->

<!-- svg: ../images/diagrams/night-timeline.svg -->

Notes: The dough is mixed late on purpose, so it can ferment slowly and cool through the small hours. Flavour comes from time, not from extra yeast.

## The long rise

Six hours of bulk fermentation, folded twice, while the whole town sleeps.

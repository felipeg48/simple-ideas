# Shape & bake

---

## Shape

<!-- svg: ../images/anim/loaf.svg shaped -->

- Tip the dough out — gentle hands, no tearing
- Fold it taut, into a long **batard**
- Rest it seam-side up in a floured basket

Notes: Shaping builds the skin tension that lets the loaf spring instead of spreading.

---

## Score & load

<!-- svg: ../images/anim/loaf.svg +scored +oven -->

- One confident slash with the **lame**
- Slide it onto the hot stone, steam in
- Close the door on 250°C

Notes: The score is where the loaf chooses to open; steam keeps the crust soft just long enough to rise.

---

## Oven spring

<!-- svg: ../images/anim/loaf.svg +baking -->
<!-- steps: +sprung +baked,-baking -->
- Ten minutes of lift — the **oven spring**
- Steam off, and the crust sets, darkens, and sings

Notes: Most of the rise happens in the first minutes of the bake, before the crust hardens and locks the shape in.

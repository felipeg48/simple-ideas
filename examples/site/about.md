We are the order platform team at Northwind Logistics. We own checkout,
payment orchestration, stock reservation and the courier hand-off, and we
carry the pager for all four.

<!-- badge: Hiring two backend engineers · info -->

## How we work

- Every incident gets a written review within five working days.
- Every review ends with one change to the lab, so the next person can break
  the same thing on purpose.
- Talks and posts on this site come from those reviews.

## Where we are

Most of the team sits in Gothenburg; three of us work from Lisbon. The
warehouse in Borås is a forty-minute drive, and we visit it during peak
season to watch the floor while the dashboards move.

# The order journey

From checkout to the doorstep in five services

Notes: Tonight we follow one order, number 4711, from the moment a customer presses Pay to the moment a van leaves our dock.

---

## Five services, one order

- Checkout takes the basket
- Payment charges the card
- Stock reserves the items
- Picking sends a person to the shelf
- The courier takes the parcel

Notes: Every order passes through the same five services, in the same order, and each one can fail on its own.
